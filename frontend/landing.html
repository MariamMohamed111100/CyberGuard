About CyberGuard

CyberGuard started with a question: why does network security need a PhD, a VPN subscription, and a weekend of YAML? So we built a self-hosted watchdog that scans your LAN on its own schedule, spots what changed, and tells you what matters in plain words.

0 cloud servers 60 sec scan cycles 1 dashboard

Security posture

Everything runs on your machine. Scans stay inside your LAN, the admin dashboard lives behind a signed session, and nothing phones home. If a feature needs admin rights, it asks for them; if it does not, it leaves your privileges alone.

Session-based auth No telemetry Local SQLite only

Privacy promise

There is no data to sell, because nothing ever leaves home. The dashboard holds device IPs, MACs and ports for your own eyes only, and your preferences live in your browser, not on a server.

Data stays local Anonymous usage The log is yours
